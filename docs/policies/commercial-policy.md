### C01  Introduction and Applicability

This Policy explains subscriptions, Job Credits, Contact Credits, payments and refunds on Avenso. It applies to Employers using Individual, Contractor or Firm accounts. Workers are not charged these platform credits under the current model.

Avenso is operated by Priyanshu Narwaria in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, India. Read this Policy with the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and Help, Grievance & Account Deletion page. Platform charges pay for access to Avenso features, not worker wages or guaranteed hiring. Employers pay Workers directly.

### C02  Subscription Plans

Subscriptions provide the Job Credits, Contact Credits and any other benefits stated in the plan offered before purchase. A plan does not guarantee worker responses, availability or completed work.

Avenso will disclose the price, included credits, duration and any applicable limits before payment.

### C03  Subscription Purchase and Renewal

Initially, Employers purchase subscriptions by contacting Avenso administration at **support@avenso.in**. Avenso provides plan details and authorized payment instructions. Credits are added after payment is verified and the purchase is approved.

Renewal is manual, not automatic. When a subscription ends, its plan-specific benefits may end, but unused allocated credits remain available while the account is active and eligible. Refunds of purchased subscriptions follow C09.

### C04  Job Credits

Job Credits allow Employers to publish jobs. A successfully published job costs **two (2) Job Credits**. Drafts and failed publication attempts do not constitute successful publication.

If credits are deducted but publication fails, Avenso will restore the two credits within 72 hours after the failed publication is identified or reported and verified. Publication charges are for posting, not for obtaining applicants or completing work. A new successful publication may require a new charge; any charge for editing or reposting will be disclosed before confirmation.

### C05  Contact Credits

In Find Worker, **one (1) Contact Credit** is charged when eligible worker contact details are successfully unlocked. Worker interest or request acceptance alone does not create a charge. Contact access does not guarantee a work arrangement.

If an eligible contact unlock fails or a duplicate charge is made for the same connection, Avenso will review the transaction and restore credits incorrectly deducted. A valid unlock is not automatically refundable because the parties do not agree on work or the Worker later becomes unavailable, subject to applicable law. Any charges for contacting applicants through Create Job, if applicable, must be shown before confirmation.

### C06  Additional Credit Purchases

Employers may purchase Job Credits or Contact Credits without purchasing a subscription, through Avenso administration. The credit type, quantity, price and payment instructions will be disclosed before purchase.

If a verified purchase is not credited correctly, contact support with the transaction details for correction or an applicable refund.

### C07  Credit Wallet

Avenso records Job Credits and Contact Credits added, used and restored to an Employer account. Users can review the balance made available in the app; backend transaction records govern corrections to errors.

Unused credits do not expire due to inactivity or subscription expiry while the account remains active and eligible. Credits are not cash and cannot be sold or transferred between accounts. Voluntary account deletion ends access to unused credits without a discretionary refund, subject to legal rights and approved refunds.

### C08  Payments and Transactions

Purchases are initially arranged through authorized Avenso administrators. Pay only using verified payment instructions; Avenso will never ask for a login OTP to receive payment. Payment screenshots alone do not establish successful settlement.

Avenso may verify and correct pending, failed or duplicate transactions. Taxes and transaction records will be handled as legally required. Worker wages are paid directly between Employer and Worker, not through Avenso.

### C09  Cancellation and Refunds

Cancelling a job or work arrangement does not automatically refund a valid platform charge. Failed job-publication deductions are restored as described in C04. Failed contact unlocks are handled under C05.

For payment problems or refund requests, email **support@avenso.in** with the purchase date, amount, transaction reference and reason. Avenso will review the request and communicate its decision. Approved monetary refunds will be processed to an appropriately verified payment account, subject to applicable payment rules. Refunds are considered for duplicate or erroneous payments, undelivered paid features and other circumstances required by law. A validly delivered platform service is not automatically refundable solely because a job or work arrangement does not proceed. Nothing in this Policy removes mandatory legal refund rights.

### C10  Special Circumstances

A Worker declining work, failing to attend or disagreeing about wages does not automatically mean the platform's contact service failed. Avenso may assist with another connection where practical, without guaranteeing a replacement.

Fraud, service errors or unauthorized credit deductions may be reviewed and corrected. During an account suspension, unused credits remain recorded but cannot be used until access is restored. If an account is reinstated, eligible remaining credits become available again. On voluntary account deletion, unused credits are forfeited subject to applicable law. Outstanding approved refunds and records that must be retained by law are unaffected.

### C11  Offers and Promotional Credits

Avenso may introduce discounts or promotional credits. Any offer will specify its eligibility, benefit, restrictions and validity before use. No particular promotion is promised by this Policy.

Purchased and subscription-allocated credits retain the no-time-expiry rule described in C07. Promotional credits, if offered, may have separately disclosed terms that comply with applicable law.

### C12  Support and Disputes

For billing, credit or refund questions, contact **support@avenso.in**. Include the relevant transaction or job reference and a short description; never share login OTPs or unnecessary bank details.

Avenso may review payment records, credit ledger entries and publication or unlock events and correct verified errors. Complaints may be submitted to the support email above. Avenso will acknowledge and investigate complaints and communicate the outcome through an appropriate channel. Users may request further review by replying to the support correspondence. Material changes to this Policy will be communicated through the app or another official channel, with an updated effective date.
