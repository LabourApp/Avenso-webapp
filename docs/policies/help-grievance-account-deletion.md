# Help, Grievance & Account Deletion

*VERSION 1.0*

Effective from: 18 November 2026  |  Employer App · Worker App

Operated by Priyanshu Narwaria, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, India. This page explains how to contact Avenso, report problems, request privacy assistance and close an account. It supplements the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and Commercial Policy. It does not replace legal remedies or emergency services.

### H01  Contact and Customer Support

For login, account, worker profile review, jobs, connections, credits, payments, technical issues or deletion assistance, email **support@avenso.in**. Include a brief description and the relevant job or transaction reference, if available. Never share login OTPs, passwords or unnecessary financial information.

### H02  Grievance Redressal

Users may submit complaints about account decisions, platform transactions, privacy concerns or conduct involving another user to **@avenso.in@gmail.com**. Avenso may request relevant evidence, review available records and communicate its decision or next steps. Personal information about other users may be withheld for privacy or safety reasons. Users retain the right to approach competent authorities or pursue remedies available under law.

### H03  Employer Complaints

Employers may report misleading worker profiles, misconduct, no-shows, connection problems or incorrect credit deductions. Include the job or connection reference and any relevant records. Avenso may investigate and, where practical, assist with another worker connection, but does not guarantee replacement or job completion. Credit reversals and refunds follow the Commercial Policy.

### H04  Worker Complaints

Workers may report misleading jobs, harassment, unsafe conditions, threats, wage disputes, non-payment or inappropriate requests for money or documents. Employers and workers agree wages and settle payments directly; Avenso does not collect or guarantee wages. Avenso may review available platform records and take appropriate account-level action, without guaranteeing wage recovery.

### H05  Reporting and Safety Issues

Fraud, impersonation, fake profiles, harassment, exploitation, unlawful work and misuse of personal information are prohibited. Report concerns by email with the relevant job or profile reference and supporting details, where safe. Avenso may review reports, remove content or restrict accounts where warranted. For immediate danger, injury or crime, contact emergency services or the police first; Avenso is not an emergency-response service.

### H06  Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion through the account-deletion option in the relevant app, where available, or by emailing **nsupport@avenso.in**. Avenso may verify control of the registered account. Active or unresolved work, disputes, refunds or legal obligations may affect when deletion can be completed.

On deletion, personal profile data is deleted or de-identified as applicable, while certain records may be retained for lawful purposes. Historical references may display “Anonymous”; this does not mean every underlying record is erased. Unused credits are forfeited on voluntary deletion, subject to applicable law and any existing refund rights. See the Privacy Policy and Commercial Policy.

### H07  Privacy Requests

For access, correction, consent withdrawal, deletion or other privacy concerns, email **support@avenso.in**. Avenso may ask for limited information to verify account control and will handle requests subject to applicable law. Withdrawing location or other necessary permissions may limit relevant app functions. Further information is available in the Privacy Policy.

### H08  Complaint Follow-up

When following up, reply to the existing support email thread and include any job, account or transaction reference previously provided. Avenso may request more information and communicate the outcome or next steps. If dissatisfied, users may request further review through the same contact channel and may exercise available legal remedies.

**Policy updates:** Avenso may update this page and communicate material changes through the apps or another appropriate official channel.
